Events

To spice up the MoFood gameplay, there are serveral events that can happen. There are two types of events in MoFood, seasonal events and annual event.

Seasonal Event List​

As the name suggest, seasonal event happens every MoFood seasons. In this type of event, it provides you with some boost or effects that will aid you doing MoFood during the event. Since the event hinges on the seasonal cycle in mofood, this event happens frequently every few irl days.

Autumn Harvest (12 September -> 2 October)​

Effects
Double Crop Yield
Bonus Seeds
+50% Mastery XP

Merchant'S Fortune (16 November -> 21 November)​

Effects
Bonus Noms from harvests
Bonus money rewards
+50% Mastery XP

Spring Fesival (10 February -> 14 February)???​

Effects
Boosted Drops

Annual Event List​

Annual Event differs from your regular seasonal events. Annual event happens once per IRL year. This kind of events is tied to real life events. Unlike seasonal event, annual events have a certain goal that you need to achieve to obtain a reward, you also can compete with other players to reach the leaderboard and server will rewards the top players on that event leaderboard at the end of the event.

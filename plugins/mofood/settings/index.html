Settings

In MoFood, there are serveral quality of life features that can help you throughout your MoFood journey. Some of this settings can be configured on/off in the MoFood setting. You can visit this menu in /food -> Player Profile -> Settings.

Enchant Settings​

Settings related to enchantments.

Anonymous Charity​

Hides your name on the charity message when you triggers the charity enchantment.

Enabled

Disabled

Hide Transmutation Alert​

Hides the message that appears when you triggers the transmutation enchantment.

Hide Green Touch Alert​

Hides the message that appears when you triggers the green touch enchantment.

Crop Setting​

Settings related to crop.

Growth Message​

Tells you the growth stage of a crop by right clicking it.

Seed Breaking​

Will stop you from breaking seeds.

Noms are the primary currency that you will be using throughout this plugin. You can view your noms via the main menu by hovering over your head!

## Obtaining Noms
Noms can be obtained through various ways! Besides the first few you start out with you can obtain them via:
- Harvesting crops
- Harvesting bonsai
- Through custom enchants like Charity
- Quest Completion

## Noms Usage
You can spend noms in almost any area of the plugin. It's best if you  start collecting them as early and as many as possible. Some of the area's you'll be spending noms on are:
- Buying seeds 
- Buying Ingredients
- Buying crop growth chambers
- Purchasing items from the Traveling Merchant

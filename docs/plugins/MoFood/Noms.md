Noms are the primary currency that you will be using throughout this plugin. You can view your noms via the main menu by hovering over your head!

## Obtaining Noms
Noms can be obtained through various ways! Besides the first few you start out with you can obtain them via:
- Harvesting crops
- Harvesting bonsai
- Through custom enchants like Charity
- Quest Completion

## Noms Usage
You can spend noms in almost any area of the plugin. It's best if you  start collecting them as early and as many as possible. Some of the area's you'll be spending noms on are:
- Buying seeds 
- Buying Ingredients
- Buying crop growth chambers
- Purchasing items from the Traveling Merchant

## Noms Pouch
If you played MoFood for a while, you might encountered something called "Noms Pouch". Noms pouch is like a loot box for noms. You cant craft it yourself, but a lot of sources use noms pouch as a reward.

If you open a noms pouch, you get a randomized amount of noms. There are diffrent sizes of noms pouch, each of them have diffrent min and max value for the amount of noms you randomly get. To open noms pouch, just right click the item, and it will do an opening animation.

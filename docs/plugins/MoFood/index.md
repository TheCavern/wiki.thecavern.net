# MoFood
MoFood is a Minecraft food plugin that overhauls farming and food preparation mechanics in Minecraft. It makes basic crop collection into a deeper, systemized experience by introducing season-dependent crops, immersive artisan good creation, and gameplay tied to harvesting and cooking, changing the boring farming gameplay into a structured progression rather than a simple resource grind.

MoFood is designed to provide an immersive farming gameplay loop. Players can grow custom crops, and then process them, and use those ingredients to cook a wider variety of consumables that can be eaten to restore hunger and sometimes provide a custom effect. True to its name, MoFood expands the available culinary content by adding many new food items that are cooked from ingredients obtained through the plugin's farming and growth systems.

MoFood includes a range of cool features that enable that depth, including custom crops (with seasonal growth), custom farming tools that can be enchanted and upgraded over time as you progress on the plugin, interactive bonsai mechanics for fruits, brewing systems that allow you to create and age brews, delivery mechanics, questlines tied to cooking/harvesting, and plans for custom animals and more forestry features.

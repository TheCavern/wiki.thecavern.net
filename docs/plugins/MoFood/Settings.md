# Settings
In MoFood, there are serveral quality of life features that can help you throughout your MoFood journey. Some of this settings can be configured on/off in the MoFood setting. You can visit this menu in `/food` -> `Player Profile` -> `Settings`.



## Enchant Settings
Settings related to enchantments.

### Anonymous Charity
Hides your name on the charity message when you triggers the [charity enchantment](https://wikidev.shinyshoe.net/plugins/mofood/farming_tool/enchantments#charity).
